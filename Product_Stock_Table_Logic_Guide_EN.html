2. Detailed Explanation of Each Column

1. OPENING — Opening Stock Balance
Represents the inventory balance available in the warehouse at 00:00 hours on the 1st day of the selected month.
• Logic: Previous month's ending Closing Stock automatically becomes this month's Opening Stock.
• Formula: Opening = Σ(Inward transactions before 1st of month) - Σ(Outward transactions before 1st of month)
2. RECEIPT — Inward Stock Receipts
Tracks all positive incoming inventory into this warehouse/location during the month:
• Goods Receive Note (GRN): Shipments purchased from vendors/manufacturers against verified Purchase Orders.
• MTN_IN (Material Transfer In): Shipments transferred into this location from Head Office (HO) or another branch.
3. SENT — Outward Stock Transfers
Tracks inventory dispatched from this location to other locations:
• MTN_OUT (Approved): Triggered when a Material Transfer Note transitions to "APPROVED" status.
• Delivery Challan (DC): Dispatches made to client sites or external locations via challans.
4. APPROVED — Service Consumption in Field Work
Tracks chemicals and consumables consumed during pest control services and AMC visits by technicians:
• SERVICE_OUT: Deducted when a service completion report is verified and approved.
• SERVICE_RETURN / REVERSAL: If any unused chemical is returned to the warehouse by the technician, it is deducted from this figure to ensure exact real-time accuracy.
Approved = Gross Chemical Used - Unused Returned Chemical
5. RESERVED — Stock on Hold (DRAFT Protection)
Inventory that is still physically inside the warehouse, but earmarked/blocked for a pending transfer:
• When an MTN is created in "DRAFT" status, the requested quantity is immediately reserved to prevent other staff from committing the same stock.
• When the MTN is "APPROVED", the reservation is released and the quantity moves into SENT.
• If the DRAFT MTN is Cancelled or Deleted, the reservation is released and returns to AVAILABLE.
6. CLOSING — Total Physical Stock on Hand
The actual physical count of units sitting on the warehouse shelves right now:
• Physical Audit Target: When warehouse managers conduct a physical shelf count, it must match this Closing figure.
• Note: Reserved stock is still physically in the building, so it is included within Closing Stock.
Closing = Opening + Receipt - Sent - Approved
7. AVAILABLE — Net Available Stock for New Operations
The most critical operational metric for dispatchers, branch managers, and technicians.
• Indicates the maximum quantity of stock that can be safely issued for new work orders or new transfer notes today.
AVAILABLE = CLOSING STOCK - RESERVED STOCK

4. Core Operational Rules for Inventory Management

  • Negative Stock Prevention: The system enforces a strict rule where transfer or issue quantities cannot exceed the Available stock quantity.
  • Draft MTN Hygiene: Never leave Draft MTNs lingering indefinitely. If a planned transfer is no longer needed, Cancel/Delete the Draft MTN immediately to return the reserved stock to the Available pool.
  • Monthly Reconciliations: Physical inventory stocktakes at month-end must always be benchmarked against the Closing Stock column.
  • Access Control: In compliance with security governance, only Admin (`role == 'admin'`) and Super Admin (`is_superuser=True`) have permissions to delete records across the system.
